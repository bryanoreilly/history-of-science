# William Harvey

## Overview

William Harvey was an English physician and anatomist who argued that the heart pumps blood through a closed circulatory system.

## Timeline

- 1578: Born in Kent.
- 1602: Receives an M.D. from the University of Padua.
- 1616: Lectures on circulation at the Royal College of Physicians.
- 1628: Publishes `De motu cordis`.
- 1657: Dies.
