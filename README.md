# History of Science

A chronological and interconnected knowledge base for studying the people, places, experiments, mathematics, discoveries, technologies, sources, and historical circumstances through which scientific knowledge developed.

The repository is the knowledge base. Books, videos, courses, lectures, and playlists are learning paths through it.

## Organizing Axes

- Time: chronology, approximate dates, periods, and sequences.
- Geography: places, institutions, expeditions, laboratories, publication sites, and coordinates when verified.
- Connections: influence, evidence, mathematical prerequisites, technological consequences, and intellectual lineage.

## Working Rule

Do not create duplicate biographical or conceptual summaries inside learning paths. A Crash Course episode, textbook chapter, or primary source note should link to canonical records such as `02-people/isaac-newton.md` or `03-discoveries-and-theories/universal-gravitation.md`.

## Status Labels

- `stub`: identified but minimally developed.
- `developing`: researched enough to be useful, still incomplete.
- `reviewed`: checked against reliable sources.
- `well-sourced`: strong citations, uncertainty addressed.

## Source Policy

Educational syntheses help us navigate. Important claims should eventually be grounded in scholarly secondary sources, academic/institutional references, or primary sources.
