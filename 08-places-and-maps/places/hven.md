# Hven

## Overview

Hven was the island where Tycho Brahe built his observatories Uraniborg and Stjerneborg.

## Historical Importance

Episode 13 presents Hven as the site of Tycho's research program, instruments, staff, printing press, paper mill, and alchemical equipment.
