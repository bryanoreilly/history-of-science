# Johannes Kepler

## Overview

Johannes Kepler used Tycho Brahe's observations to reform Copernican astronomy. Episode 13 focuses on `Astronomia nova`, Mars, elliptical orbits, and the first two laws of planetary motion.

## Timeline

- 1571: Born near Stuttgart.
- 1591: Receives M.A. at the University of Tubingen.
- 1594: Begins teaching mathematics in Graz.
- 1600: Moves to Prague to work with Tycho Brahe.
- 1601: Succeeds Tycho as Imperial Mathematician.
- 1604: Observes a new star.
- 1609: Publishes `Astronomia nova`, containing the first two planetary laws.
- 1619: Publishes `Harmonice Mundi`, containing the third law.
- 1630: Dies in Regensburg.
