# Tycho's 1572 New Star Observation

## Question

Was the "new star" a sublunary phenomenon, a comet, or a change in the supposedly unchanging heavens?

## Observations

Tycho observed a new star in Cassiopeia in 1572 and published `De nova stella` the following year.

## Scientific Impact

The observation helped challenge the idea that the heavens were perfect and unchanging.
