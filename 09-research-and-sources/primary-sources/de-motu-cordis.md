# De motu cordis

## Bibliographic Information

- Full title: `Exercitatio Anatomica de Motu Cordis et Sanguinis in Animalibus`
- Author: William Harvey
- Published: 1628

## Purpose

Primary source for Harvey's theory that the heart pumps blood in a closed circulatory system.
