# Telescope

## Overview

The telescope enabled Galileo's early astronomical observations of the Moon, Jupiter's satellites, Venus, Saturn, and other celestial phenomena. Episode 13 emphasizes Galileo's refinement and astronomical use of the instrument beginning in 1609.

## Scientific Prerequisites

- Optics
- Lens grinding

## Inventors / Developers

- Galileo Galilei refined and used the telescope for astronomy; he did not invent it.
