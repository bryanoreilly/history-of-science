# Rene Descartes

## Overview

Rene Descartes was a French philosopher and mathematician associated with methodological doubt, deductive reasoning, mechanical philosophy, and the mathematical representation of space.

## Timeline

- 1596: Born in La Haye en Touraine.
- 1633: Suppresses publication of `Le Monde` after Galileo's trial.
- 1637: Publishes `Discourse on Method`.
- 1650: Dies in Stockholm.
