# Antonie van Leeuwenhoek

## Overview

Antonie van Leeuwenhoek was a Dutch microscopist whose lens work and observations revealed forms of life too small to be seen by the unaided eye.

## Timeline

- 1623: Born in Delft.
- 1673: Microscope-enabled observations are published by the Royal Society.
- 1677: Observes spermatozoa.
- 1682: Observes muscle fibers.
- 1723: Dies.
