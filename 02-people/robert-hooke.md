# Robert Hooke

## Overview

Robert Hooke was an English natural philosopher, microscopist, and Royal Society curator of experiments whose `Micrographia` made microscope-enabled observation a major public scientific form.

## Timeline

- 1635: Born on the Isle of Wight.
- 1665: Publishes `Micrographia`.
- 1703: Dies.
