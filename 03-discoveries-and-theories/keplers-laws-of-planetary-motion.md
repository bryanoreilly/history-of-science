# Kepler's Laws of Planetary Motion

## Summary

Kepler's planetary laws described planetary motion using ellipses and variable speed rather than perfect circles and uniform motion. The first two laws appeared in `Astronomia nova` in 1609; the third appeared in `Harmonice Mundi` in 1619.
