# Jan Swammerdam

## Overview

Jan Swammerdam was a Dutch natural philosopher who studied animal and plant tissues with microscopes and made important observations about insect development and blood.

## Timeline

- 1637: Born in Amsterdam.
- 1658: Observes red blood cells.
- 1680: Dies.
