# Columbian Exchange

## Summary

The Columbian Exchange was the post-1492 movement of plants, animals, diseases, people, technologies, and ideas between the Americas and Afro-Eurasia.

## Historical Context

The exchange was inseparable from European colonization, Indigenous population collapse, forced labor, plantation economies, and global trade.
