# Stable Identifiers

Use stable IDs for machine-readable records.

```text
PER-0001 Person
CON-0001 Discovery, theory, or concept
EXP-0001 Experiment
TEC-0001 Technology
MAT-0001 Mathematics
PLC-0001 Place or institution
SRC-0001 Source
```

IDs should not be reused after deletion or major reclassification.
