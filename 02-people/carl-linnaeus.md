# Carl Linnaeus

## Overview

Carl Linnaeus was a Swedish naturalist whose classification work helped organize botanical and zoological knowledge through systematic naming and comparison.

## Timeline

- 1707: Born.
- 1735: Publishes the first edition of `Systema Naturae`.
- 1753: Publishes `Species Plantarum`.
- 1778: Dies.
