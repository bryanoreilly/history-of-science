# Galileo's Jupiter Satellite Observations

## Question

Did all celestial bodies orbit Earth, or could there be other centers of motion?

## Observations

Galileo observed small bodies near Jupiter and determined that they orbited Jupiter.

## Scientific Impact

The observations did not by themselves prove Copernicanism, but they supplied evidence against a simple Earth-only center of motion.
