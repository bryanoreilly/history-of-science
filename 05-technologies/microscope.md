# Microscope

## Overview

The microscope made structures and organisms too small for unaided sight available for investigation, helping create new practices in anatomy, physiology, and microbiology.

## Scientific Prerequisites

- Lens making
- Optics

## Inventors / Developers

- Antonie van Leeuwenhoek made powerful single-lens microscopes and used them in extensive observations.
- Robert Hooke used microscopy and printed illustrations to communicate observations to a wide audience.
