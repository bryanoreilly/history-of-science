# Tycho Brahe

## Overview

Tycho Brahe was a Danish nobleman and astronomer whose observational program became one of the foundations for Kepler's mathematical astronomy. Episode 13 emphasizes his island observatories, carefully calibrated instruments, and precise naked-eye observations.

## Timeline

- 1546: Born in Skane.
- 1566: Loses part of his nose in a duel.
- 1572: Observes a new star in Cassiopeia.
- 1573: Publishes `De nova stella`.
- 1597: Leaves Denmark after conflict with Christian IV.
- 1599: Settles in Prague as Imperial Mathematician.
- 1600: Hires Johannes Kepler as an assistant.
- 1601: Dies in Prague.
