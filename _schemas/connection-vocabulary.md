# Connection Vocabulary

Use typed relationships instead of generic related-to links.

## Relationship Types

- influenced
- built-upon
- challenged
- confirmed
- disproved
- predicted
- experimentally-demonstrated
- mathematically-enabled
- technologically-enabled
- led-to
- contemporary-of
- collaborated-with
- studied-under
- taught
- worked-at
- published

## Edge Format

```text
source-id | relationship | target-id | evidence | source
```
