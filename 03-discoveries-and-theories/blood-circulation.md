# Blood Circulation

## Summary

Blood circulation is the theory that the heart pumps blood through a closed system of arteries and veins.

## What Came Before?

- Galenic accounts in which blood was produced by the liver and diffused through the body.

## Evidence

- Observation of beating hearts.
- Comparative anatomy.
- Estimates of heart capacity, volume moved per beat, and pulse rate.
- One-way valves in blood vessels.
