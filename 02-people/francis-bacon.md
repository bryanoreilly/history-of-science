# Francis Bacon

## Overview

Francis Bacon was an English philosopher and statesman associated with a practical, experimental, and organized approach to natural knowledge. His work helped shape later ideals of collective research, empirical investigation, and useful knowledge.

## Timeline

- 1561: Born in London.
- 1620: Publishes `Novum Organum`.
- 1626: Dies.
