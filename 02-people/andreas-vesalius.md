# Andreas Vesalius

## Overview

Andreas Vesalius was a Flemish anatomist and physician whose direct dissections and richly illustrated anatomical publication transformed early modern anatomy.

## Timeline

- 1514: Born in Brussels.
- 1537: Receives his medical degree at Padua and is appointed professor of surgery and anatomy.
- 1543: Publishes `De humani corporis fabrica`.
- 1564: Dies.
