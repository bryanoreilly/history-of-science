# Glossary

## Canonical Record

The single primary page for a person, place, theory, experiment, technology, mathematical idea, or source.

## Learning Path

A guided sequence through the knowledge base, usually based on a course, video series, book, playlist, or custom curriculum.

## Connection

A typed relationship between two records, such as influenced, confirmed, mathematically-enabled, or led-to.

## Historical Uncertainty

Any condition where date, attribution, priority, interpretation, or source reliability is unclear, approximate, disputed, or traditional rather than firmly established.
